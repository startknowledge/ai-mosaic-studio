Common Mistakes in Data Management (And How Mosaic Fixes Them)

Even with advanced tools like Mosaic, missteps can undermine success. Here are five critical errors and how to avoid them.

1. Ignoring Data Quality

Mistake: Assuming "clean data" is already in place. Problem: Garbage in, garbage out (GIGO). Poor data leads to wrong insights and bad decisions.

Mosaic Fix:

  • Automated data profiling detects anomalies.
  • AI suggests fixes (e.g., "This field has 30% null values—should we impute or flag?").
  • Real-time validation ensures only clean data flows into analytics.

How to Avoid: ✅ Run Mosaic’s "Data Health Check" before full integration.

2. Over-Reliance on Static Reports

Mistake: Using one-time dashboards instead of dynamic insights. Problem: Reports become obsolete by the time they’re read.

Mosaic Fix:

  • AI-generated narratives update in real time.
  • Interactive storytelling lets users explore "what-if" scenarios.

How to Avoid: ✅ Replace at least 50% of static reports with Mosaic’s AI summaries.

3. Poor Governance & Compliance Risks

Mistake: Treating data governance as an afterthought. Problem: Fines, breaches, and reputational damage can cripple a business.

Mosaic Fix:

  • Automatic data classification (PII, sensitive, public).

How to Avoid: ✅ Conduct a GDPR/CCPA readiness assessment using Mosaic’s governance tools.

Mistake: Assuming employees will automatically adapt to new tech. Problem: Low adoption means Mosaic becomes a wasted investment.
