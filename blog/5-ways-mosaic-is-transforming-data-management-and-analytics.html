How Mosaic Solves It: AI-Powered Data Cleaning & Compliance

Mosaic automates data governance with: 🔹 Automated deduplication (finds and merges duplicate entries). 🔹 Schema enforcement (ensures all data follows the same structure). 🔹 Compliance checks (flags PII violations before they happen). 🔹 Audit trails (tracks who accessed/modified data).
