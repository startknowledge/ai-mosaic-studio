Common Mistakes When Adopting Mosaic (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the best tools can fail if implemented poorly. Here are five pitfalls and how to prevent them:

❌ Mistake 1: Skipping Data Cleaning Before Integration

Problem: Dirty data (duplicates, missing values, inconsistencies) poisons your analytics. Solution:

  • Clean data before loading into Mosaic.
  • Use Mosaic’s built-in data quality checks to flag issues.

❌ Mistake 2: Overlooking User Training

Problem: If teams don’t know how to use Mosaic, they’ll stick to old methods. Solution:

  • Provide hands-on training (videos, live sessions).
  • Assign a "data coach" to guide early adopters.

❌ Mistake 3: Not Defining Clear Use Cases

Problem: Without a specific goal, Mosaic becomes just another tool. Solution:

  • Start with 1-2 high-impact use cases (e.g., fraud detection, demand forecasting).

❌ Mistake 5: Underestimating Change Management
