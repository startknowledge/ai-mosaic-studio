8 Actionable Strategies to Create AI-Generated Art Like Dolores González

If you’re inspired by Dolores González’s work and want to create your own AI-powered digital mosaics, here are eight proven strategies to get started—whether you're a beginner or an experienced artist.

Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Concept Before Running AI

Problem: Many artists jump straight into AI tools without a clear vision, leading to generic, uninspired outputs.

Solution: Dolores González’s process begins with a deep conceptual foundation. Before generating anything, ask:

  • What emotion or message do I want to convey?
  • What cultural or historical references should I incorporate?
  • What color palettes and textures will best represent my idea?

Example: González once created an AI mosaic titled "Echoes of the Maya" by studying ancient Mayan glyphs, jungle landscapes, and geometric patterns. She fed the AI high-resolution images of Mayan artifacts alongside modern digital textures to ensure the output felt both ancient and futuristic.

Action Step:

  1. Sketch a rough concept (even a simple doodle).
  2. Research historical, cultural, or personal references that inspire you.
  3. Use tools like Miro or Figma to organize your ideas before running AI.

Strategy 2: Use MidJourney or Stable Diffusion for High-Quality Base Images

Problem: Free AI art generators often produce low-resolution or overly stylized images that lack depth.

Solution: Dolores González prefers MidJourney and Stable Diffusion because they offer:

  • Higher resolution outputs (up to 1024x1024 or higher).
  • More control over style (e.g., "cyberpunk mosaic," "neoclassical fractal").
  • Better texture generation (important for digital mosaics).

Example: For her piece "Neon Serenity," González used MidJourney with the prompt: "A digital mosaic of a woman in a futuristic garden, cyberpunk style, ultra-detailed, 8K, intricate patterns, mosaic tiles, vibrant neon colors"

She then refined the image in Photoshop, adjusting colors and adding manual brushwork for a hybrid effect.

Action Step:

  • Experiment with different AI models (MidJourney, DALL·E 3, Stable Diffusion).
  • Use negative prompts to avoid unwanted elements (e.g., "blurry, low quality").
  • Generate multiple variations and pick the best one.

Strategy 3: Train a Custom AI Model on Your Own Artwork

Problem: Generic AI models struggle to replicate your unique artistic style.

Solution: Dolores González fine-tunes AI models using her own work. This ensures the AI understands her aesthetic and can generate coherent, personalized outputs.

How to Do It:

  1. Collect a dataset of your best artwork (at least 50–100 high-res images).
  2. Use Stable Diffusion’s "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) or MidJourney’s custom training to train the model.
  3. Test the model with new prompts to see if it captures your style.

Example: González trained a custom Stable Diffusion model on her digital mosaic portraits. When she prompted "a woman with a floral mosaic face," the AI automatically incorporated her signature patterns rather than generic styles.

Action Step:

  • Start with free tools like Automatic1111’s Stable Diffusion WebUI for LoRA training.
  • If training is too complex, use pre-trained style models (e.g., "Deliberate" for artistic styles).

Strategy 4: Combine AI with Manual Brushwork for a Hybrid Approach

Problem: Pure AI-generated art can sometimes feel too mechanical or lacking in soul.

Solution: Dolores González uses AI as a foundation but finishes pieces manually to add emotional depth and texture.

Example: For "Fractal Whispers," she:

  1. Generated a base AI image of a fractal pattern.
  2. Used Photoshop’s brush tools to enhance certain elements (e.g., adding gold leaf effects).
  3. Added hand-drawn lines to break up the digital grid, making it feel more organic.

Action Step:

  • Generate an AI image, then export it to Photoshop/Procreate.
  • Use layer masks to blend AI and manual elements seamlessly.
  • Experiment with textures (e.g., adding grain, halftone patterns).

Strategy 5: Play with AI-Powered Color Palettes

Problem: Many artists struggle to create harmonious color schemes in digital art.

Solution: Dolores González uses AI color palette generators to automatically suggest complementary colors based on her themes.

Tools to Try:

  • Coolors (AI-generated palettes)
  • Adobe Color (AI-enhanced color schemes)
  • DALL·E’s color description features (e.g., "a mosaic in shades of emerald and gold")

Example: For "Oceanic Mosaic," she used an AI tool to generate a blue-green-teal palette, then applied it to a wave-like digital mosaic for a calming, underwater effect.

Action Step:

  1. Pick a theme (e.g., "forest," "space," "urban").
  2. Use an AI color tool to generate palettes.
  3. Test the colors in your AI-generated image before finalizing.

Strategy 6: Use AI to Generate Endless Variations of a Single Design

Problem: Artists often get stuck in creative ruts because they rely on the same techniques.

Solution: Dolores González lets AI generate multiple variations of a single concept, then selects the best ones to refine.

Example: For her "Mosaic of Faces" series, she:

  1. Prompted the AI: "A digital mosaic portrait of a woman, 10 different styles, intricate patterns, high detail."
  2. Generated 12 variations in one go.
  3. Chose the most balanced composition and expanded it into a full piece.

Action Step:

  • Batch-generate images using AI’s "multiple images" feature (MidJourney, DALL·E).
  • Sort by "interestingness" (MidJourney’s ranking system).
  • Combine elements from different variations into one final piece.

Strategy 7: Incorporate Cultural and Historical References

Problem: AI art can sometimes feel generic or disconnected from real-world meaning.

Solution: Dolores González deeply researches cultural and historical motifs before generating AI art.

Example: For "Aztec Cosmos," she:

  1. Studied Aztec codices and celestial maps.
  2. Prompted the AI: "A digital mosaic of the Aztec sun god, Huitzilopochtli, with fractal patterns inspired by ancient manuscripts, ultra-detailed, 8K."

  • Visit museums or read books on the culture you want to explore.
  • Use AI to generate base images, then add manual elements for authenticity.

Strategy 8: Experiment with AI-Generated 3D Textures for Depth

Problem: Flat digital mosaics can lack dimension.

Solution: Dolores González uses AI to generate 3D textures, then applies them to her mosaics for a tactile, realistic effect.

Tools to Try:

  • MidJourney’s "3D render" prompts (e.g., "a mosaic tile with a bumpy texture, hyper-realistic, 8K.")
  • Blender + AI (generate textures in AI, import into Blender for 3D modeling).
  • Substance Designer (for advanced texture creation).

Example: For "Volcanic Mosaic," she:

  1. Generated a 3D-rendered lava texture in MidJourney.
  2. Applied it to a digital mosaic pattern in Photoshop.
  3. Added lighting effects to simulate depth.

Action Step:

  • Generate a high-res texture in AI.
  • Import it into a 3D software (Blender, ZBrush).
  • Combine with 2D mosaics for a hybrid 3D/2D effect.
