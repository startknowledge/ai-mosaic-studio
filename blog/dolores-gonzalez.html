Common Mistakes When Using AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most innovative artists make mistakes when working with AI. Here are five pitfalls and how González avoids them.

1. Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Touch

Mistake: Generating art entirely with AI and skipping manual refinement. Solution: González always edits AI outputs—whether by adjusting colors, adding details, or combining with traditional techniques.

How to fix it:

  • Never publish AI art "as-is"—always refine it.
  • Use AI for inspiration, not final output.
  • Blend AI with human creativity for uniqueness.

2. Ignoring Copyright and Ethical Concerns

Mistake: Using AI to generate art that violates copyright (e.g., replicating famous artists’ styles without permission). Solution: González avoids direct copies and instead inspires AI with original concepts.

How to fix it:

  • Use AI for original ideas, not exact replicas.
  • Credit AI tools if required (some platforms have usage policies).
  • Check AI-generated art for biases (e.g., racial, gender stereotypes).

3. Poor Prompt Engineering

Mistake: Giving AI vague or unclear prompts, leading to irrelevant outputs. Solution: González crafts detailed, structured prompts with specific details.

How to fix it:

  • Be specific: Instead of "a cat", try "a cyberpunk cat with neon eyes, standing on a floating platform, ultra-detailed, 4K".
  • Use examples: Reference styles ("like a mix of Studio Ghibli and cyberpunk").
  • Iterate: If the first try is off, refine the prompt.

4. Neglecting Technical Optimization

Mistake: Generating low-quality, slow-loading AI art that hurts user experience. Solution: González optimizes AI outputs for web and print.

How to fix it:

  • Compress images (use WebP format).
  • Test loading speeds (use Google PageSpeed Insights).
  • Adjust resolutions (8K for prints, 1080p for web).

5. Not Experimenting Enough

Mistake: Sticking to one AI tool or style, missing out on innovation. Solution: González tests multiple AI platforms to find the best fit.

How to fix it:

  • Try different AI generators (DALL·E, MidJourney, Stable Diffusion).
  • Experiment with styles (surrealism, realism, abstract).
  • Stay updated on new AI art tools.
