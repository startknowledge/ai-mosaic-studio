Strategy 1: The "Prompt Engineering Blueprint" – How to Get AI to Understand Your Vision

Problem: Many artists struggle because their AI-generated art doesn’t match their mental image.

Dolores’s Solution: AI doesn’t "see" like humans—it interprets text prompts. The key is structured, descriptive prompting.

Step-by-Step Prompting Framework

  1. Start with a Clear Concept – Instead of "a fantasy dragon," try:

    "A cyberpunk dragon with neon-blue scales, glowing red eyes, and a metallic spine, standing on a floating island in a stormy digital ocean, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, 8K, trending on ArtStation."

  2. Use Negative Prompts – Exclude unwanted elements:

    "No blurry edges, no cartoonish features, no low resolution."

  3. Layer Styles & Techniques – Combine multiple artistic influences:

    "Mix the hyper-realism of Alexey Brodovitch with the surrealism of Zdzisław Beksiński, using oil paint textures."

  4. Refine with Iterations – AI improves with feedback loops:
    • Generate → Analyze → Adjust prompt → Regenerate.

Real-World Example: A student in Dolores’s course wanted an "ancient alien city" but kept getting generic sci-fi buildings. She applied the prompt blueprint and got:

"A lost city of crystalline towers, glowing with bioluminescent vines, floating platforms, 17th-century architectural details fused with alien geometry, ultra-high detail, 3D-rendered, inspired by H.R. Giger and M.C. Escher."

Result: The final piece was sold to a major gaming studio for concept art.

Strategy 2: Style Transfer Mastery – How to Make AI Mimic Any Artist

Problem: AI art often lacks distinctive style.

Dolores’s Solution: Use pre-trained style transfer models (like StyleGAN, NVIDIA’s NVIDIA StyleGAN2) to inject artistic signatures into AI outputs.

How to Do It:

  1. Find a Reference Image – Pick an artist whose style you admire (e.g., Albrecht Dürer, Yayoi Kusama, or Moebius).
  2. Use a Style Transfer Tool (e.g., DeepArt.io, Runway ML, or Adobe Firefly).
  3. Blend with Your Concept – Example prompt:

    "A cyberpunk samurai in the style of Hokusai’s woodblock prints, but with neon cybernetic enhancements, ultra-detailed, 8K."

  4. Post-Process in Photoshop – Adjust colors, textures, and lighting to refine the style.

Real-World Example: A student wanted to create a "steampunk Van Gogh" but struggled with color harmony. Dolores taught her to:
