Strategy 1: The "Prompt Engineering" Framework (How to Get AI to Understand Your Vision)

Problem: Many users struggle to get consistent, high-quality results from AI because their prompts are vague or overly complex.

Dolores’s Solution: She uses a structured prompt formula called "The 5-Part Prompt" to ensure clarity:

  1. Subject – What is the main focus?
    • Example: "A cyberpunk woman with neon tattoos"
  2. Style & Medium – How should it look?
    • Example: "In the style of Zdzisław Beksiński, hyper-detailed digital painting"
  3. Mood & Atmosphere – What emotions should it evoke?
    • Example: "Dark, mysterious, with a futuristic glow"
  4. Composition & Details – What specific elements are needed?
    • Example: "Close-up of her face, glowing eyes, intricate circuit-like tattoos"
  5. Negative Prompts (What to Avoid) – What should NOT be included?
    • Example: "No blurry edges, no cartoonish features, no realistic skin texture"

Real-World Example: A client wanted an AI-generated album cover for a dark fantasy band. Dolores used: "A medieval knight holding a glowing sword, in the style of Moebius, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, dark fantasy atmosphere, no modern elements, negative prompt: 'realistic, blurry, cartoonish'"

The result? A stunning, marketable piece that the band used for their debut single.

Action Step: Next time you use an AI tool, break your prompt into these 5 parts—you’ll see instant improvements in output quality.

Strategy 2: Layering AI with Traditional Techniques (The Hybrid Approach)

Problem: Pure AI art can sometimes look too digital or unnatural.

Dolores’s Solution: She combines AI-generated elements with hand-drawn or painted details for a unique hybrid style. Here’s how:

  2. Refine with Traditional Tools – Overlay hand-drawn textures, brushwork, or shading.
  3. Blend in Photoshop – Use layer masks and blending modes to merge AI and manual work seamlessly.

Real-World Example: For a client’s book cover, Dolores:

  • Hand-painted a character’s face in watercolor.

Action Step: Try this with your next project:

  • Sketch or paint a key element by hand.
  • Blend them in Photoshop or Procreate.
