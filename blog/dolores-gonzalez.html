How to apply it:

  • Step 1: Begin with a rough sketch (even if it’s messy).
  • Step 2: Use AI tools like MidJourney or Stable Diffusion to refine proportions, colors, and details.
  • Step 3: Manually adjust the final output to retain your artistic signature.

Real-world example: A client once approached González with a vague concept—a "fantasy city at dusk." Instead of jumping into AI, she:

  1. Drew a quick pencil sketch of the city’s layout.

  3. Selected the best AI output and hand-painted the final details, resulting in a hybrid masterpiece that felt both AI-generated and deeply personal.

Common Mistakes in AI Art (And How Dolores González Avoids Them)

Even the best AI artists make mistakes. Here are five pitfalls and how González solves them.

1. Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Touch

Mistake: Generating art entirely in AI and calling it "done." González’s fix:

  • Always add manual elements (e.g., hand-painted textures, custom backgrounds).
  • Example: She never uses AI for full compositions—instead, she uses AI for elements (e.g., backgrounds, textures) and manually arranges them.

2. Ignoring Copyright and Ethical AI Use

Mistake: Using AI models trained on copyrighted art without permission. González’s fix:

  • Only uses open-source datasets (e.g., LAION-5B).
  • Credits original artists when possible.
  • Avoids "AI deepfakes" of real people without consent.

3. Poor Prompting Leads to Generic Outputs

Mistake: Using vague prompts like "a cat" instead of "a cyberpunk cat with neon eyes, ultra-detailed, trending on ArtStation." González’s fix:

  • Uses the "5W Method" (Who, What, Where, When, Why) in prompts.
  • Example: "A lone samurai in a neon Tokyo at midnight, rain-soaked streets, cinematic lighting, inspired by Hayao Miyazaki."

4. Not Experimenting Enough

Mistake: Sticking to one AI tool or style. González’s fix:

  • Tests 3-5 different AI tools per project.
  • Mixes styles (e.g., surrealism + cyberpunk).
  • Example: She rarely repeats the same workflow—each project is a new experiment.

5. Neglecting SEO and Monetization

Mistake: Creating AI art without a strategy for sales or exposure. González’s fix:

  • Optimizes AI art for SEO (e.g., keywords in filenames, alt text).
  • Sells on multiple platforms (NFTs, prints, digital downloads).
  • Example: She tags her AI art with trending hashtags (#AIGeneratedArt, #DigitalArt) to boost discoverability.
