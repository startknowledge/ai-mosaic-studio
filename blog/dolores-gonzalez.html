1. Master Prompt Engineering: The Art of Guiding AI Creativity

Common Mistakes in AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most talented artists make mistakes when working with AI. Here are five pitfalls and how Dolores Gonzalez avoids them:

1. Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Touch

Mistake: Generating art purely from AI without any manual intervention leads to cold, robotic-looking pieces. Fix: Always refine, texture, or color-correct AI outputs. Dolores often paints over AI sketches to add warmth.

2. Ignoring Copyright and Ethical AI Use

Mistake: Using AI models trained on copyrighted data (e.g., DALL·E trained on Instagram images). Fix: Stick to open-source models (Stable Diffusion, Leonardo.AI) or ethically sourced datasets.

3. Poor Prompting Leads to Generic Art

Mistake: Vague prompts like "a cat" result in thousands of identical outputs. Fix: Be specific, descriptive, and emotional. Dolores’s prompts often include literary references ("a cat like in Edgar Allan Poe’s ‘The Black Cat,’ but in a cyberpunk alley").

4. Not Testing AI Outputs Across Devices

Mistake: AI art that looks great on a PC screen may pixelate or distort on mobile. Fix: Always export in multiple resolutions and test on different devices.

5. Failing to Document AI Art Process

Mistake: Not keeping records of prompts, models, and settings makes reproduction impossible. Fix: Use AI prompt logs (Notion, Google Docs) to track every step.
