Common Mistakes in AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most talented artists make AI-related pitfalls. Here’s how to steer clear of them:

❌ Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Touch

Problem: Some artists let AI do everything, leading to cold, robotic-looking art.

Solution:

  • Use AI as a tool, not a replacement.
  • Manually refine the best AI-generated outputs.

González’s Rule: "AI should enhance, not replace, human creativity."

❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring Copyright and Ethical AI Use

Problem: Some artists use AI without crediting sources, leading to legal issues.

Solution:

  • Always credit AI-generated elements if using public datasets.
  • Avoid deepfake controversies by disclosing AI-assisted work.

Example: A designer used AI to generate a celebrity portrait without permission. The celebrity’s team demanded removal, causing a public backlash.

González’s Advice: "Transparency builds trust—always disclose AI use."

❌ Mistake 3: Poor Prompting Leads to Generic Art

Problem: Vague prompts (like "make me a cool picture") result in uninspired outputs.

Solution:

  • Be specific—describe style, mood, and details.
  • Use negative prompts (e.g., "no blurry edges, no cartoonish faces").

Example: Instead of: ❌ "Generate a city at night." Try: ⭕ "A cyberpunk neon city at midnight, with holographic billboards, rain reflecting streetlights, and a lone drone hovering over a rooftop. Ultra HD, cinematic lighting, inspired by Blade Runner 2049."

❌ Mistake 4: Not Experimenting with Different AI Tools

Problem: Sticking to one AI model limits creativity.

Solution:

  • Test multiple tools (Stable Diffusion, MidJourney, DALL·E 3, etc.).
  • Combine them for unique effects.

González’s Workflow:

  • Stable Diffusion for sketches.
  • MidJourney for detailed renders.
  • Runway ML for video effects.

❌ Mistake 5: Neglecting Post-Processing

Problem: AI-generated art often needs manual tweaks for polish.

Solution:

  • Use Photoshop, Procreate, or Krita to refine details.
  • Adjust colors, sharpness, and composition for professional results.

Example: An AI-generated portrait had soft edges. González:

  • Used Photoshop’s AI-powered sharpening to enhance details.
  • Manually painted over the background for depth.
