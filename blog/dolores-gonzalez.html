8 Actionable Strategies to Harness AI Like Dolores González

Inspired by González’s methods, here are eight practical strategies to integrate AI into your creative workflow—whether you're an artist, designer, or developer.

1. Start with a Strong Concept Before Using AI

González’s process begins with a clear artistic vision, not the other way around. AI tools like MidJourney, DALL·E, or Stable Diffusion can generate stunning images, but without a strong concept, the output will lack depth.

How to Apply This:

  • Define your emotional or thematic goal before inputting prompts.
  • Example: If you want to create a cyberpunk cityscape, instead of just typing "cyberpunk city", refine it:
    • "A neon-lit cyberpunk city at dusk, with holographic billboards displaying futuristic ads, inspired by Blade Runner 2049, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting"
  • Use AI as a collaborator, not a replacement for your artistic intent.

2. Combine AI with Traditional Techniques for Hybrid Art

González often starts with AI-generated sketches and then refines them manually using traditional media like watercolors, ink, or digital brushes.

How to Apply This:

  • Generate a rough AI draft (e.g., using Runway ML).
  • Export it as a base layer in Photoshop or Procreate.
  • Overlay hand-painted textures to add human emotion and imperfection.
  • Example: Her "Neural Dreams" series begins with AI-generated surreal landscapes, which she then paints over with acrylic washes to create a hybrid digital-analog effect.

3. Use AI for Mood Boards and Inspiration

Before finalizing a project, González feeds AI with references (colors, styles, artists) to generate mood boards that spark new ideas.

How to Apply This:

  • Upload a palette or artwork to tools like Artbreeder or DeepDream Generator.
  • Ask AI to "mix" styles (e.g., "combine Van Gogh’s brushstrokes with cyberpunk aesthetics").
  • Use the output as a starting point for your own work.
  • Example: She once used AI to blend Picasso’s cubism with futuristic architecture, leading to a new abstract series that won critical acclaim.

4. Experiment with AI-Powered Soundscapes

González’s "DreamWeaver" project shows how AI can generate music and visuals in sync. Even if you’re not a musician, you can use AI to create atmospheric soundscapes that enhance your art.

How to Apply This:

  • Use tools like AIVA or Amper Music to compose background scores.
  • Sync visuals with AI-generated music (e.g., "a slow, melancholic piano piece with swirling abstract visuals").
  • Example: She once created a short film where AI-generated ambient sounds evolved in real-time with AI-generated visuals, resulting in a mesmerizing, immersive experience.

5. Leverage AI for Automated Color Grading

Color is critical in art, and AI can analyze and enhance your palette automatically.

How to Apply This:

How to Apply This:

Common Mistakes When Using AI in Art (And How to Avoid Them)

While AI is a powerful tool, many artists make critical errors that limit its potential. Here’s what to avoid:

1. Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Input

Mistake: Some artists let AI generate everything, leading to generic, soulless art. Solution: Always refine AI outputs with your own touch—whether through painting, editing, or storytelling.

Example: González never uses AI as a "black box"—she understands how it works and guides it intentionally.

2. Ignoring Copyright and Ethical Concerns

Mistake: Some AI tools train on copyrighted art, leading to legal risks. Solution:

  • Use open-source AI models (e.g., Stable Diffusion’s official version).
  • Credit sources when using AI-generated elements.
  • Example: González only uses AI trained on public-domain or licensed datasets to avoid legal issues.

3. Not Experimenting with Different AI Tools

Mistake: Sticking to one AI tool limits creativity. Solution: Try multiple platforms (e.g., MidJourney for images, AIVA for music, Runway for video). Example: She rotates between tools to find the best fit for each project—no single AI does everything perfectly.

4. Skipping the "Why" Behind the Art

Mistake: Generating AI art without a deeper meaning makes it feel empty. Solution: Always ask:

  • What emotion do I want to convey?
  • What story does this piece tell? Example: Her "Neural Dreams" series wasn’t just beautiful—it explored the intersection of human subconscious and machine logic.

5. Not Monetizing AI Art Strategically

Mistake: Treating AI art as free or disposable. Solution:

  • Sell digital prints, NFTs, or licensing rights.
  • Offer "AI-assisted" and "fully manual" versions for different budgets. Example: González creates tiered pricing, allowing collectors to own both AI-generated and handcrafted versions.
