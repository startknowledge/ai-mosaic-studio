8 Actionable Strategies to Apply Dolores González’s AI Art Techniques

Dolores doesn’t just theorize—she practices what she preaches. Below are eight proven strategies you can use to elevate your creative work with AI, inspired by her methods.

1. Start with a Strong Foundation: Human Sketching Before AI Refinement

Why it works: AI excels at enhancing and refining, but it struggles with original concepts. Dolores’ approach is to sketch manually first, then use AI to polish and expand the idea.

How to apply it:

  • Step 1: Create a rough digital or hand-drawn sketch (even if it’s messy).
  • Step 2: Use an AI tool like MidJourney or DALL·E 3 to generate variations based on your sketch.
  • Step 3: Select the best AI-generated elements and combine them with your original sketch for a hybrid masterpiece.

Example: A graphic designer at Nike used this method to create a new sneaker concept. They sketched a futuristic running shoe, then fed the sketch into an AI to generate textured, high-detail variations. The final design was award-winning at the 2023 Cannes Lions Festival.

2. Master Generative Prompt Engineering (Like a Pro)

Dolores believes that the quality of your AI output depends 80% on your prompt. A poorly written prompt leads to blurry, irrelevant, or generic images, while a well-structured prompt produces professional-grade art.

How to write high-concept prompts (her method): ✅ Be specific – Instead of "a futuristic city", try:

"A cyberpunk neon cityscape at dusk, inspired by Blade Runner 2049, with holographic billboards, floating taxis, and a lone figure in a high-tech trench coat, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, 8K, trending on ArtStation"

✅ Use modifiers – Add style references (e.g., "in the style of Zdzisław Beksiński"). ✅ Incorporate mood & atmosphere – Words like "dramatic," "whimsical," or "minimalist" change the output drastically. ✅ Test and refine – Run 3-5 variations and pick the best one.

Real-world example: A branding agency used Dolores’ prompt techniques to rebrand a luxury watch company. Their initial prompts were vague, leading to generic watch designs. After refining, they generated a sleek, futuristic timepiece that became a best-seller, increasing sales by 40% in 6 months.

3. Use AI for Concept Development (Not Just Execution)

Dolores often uses AI as a "brainstorming partner" before finalizing a project. She’ll generate multiple concepts, then select the strongest one to develop further.

How to apply this:

  • Step 1: Input a broad idea (e.g., "a fantasy creature").
  • Step 2: Let AI generate 10-20 variations.
  • Step 3: Pick the most intriguing one and develop it manually (e.g., sketching, animating, or writing a story around it).

Example: A video game studio used this method to design a new NPC (non-player character). Instead of starting from scratch, they fed AI keywords like "mysterious forest guardian, ancient magic, glowing eyes" and selected the most compelling version. The character became a fan favorite in their latest game.

4. Combine Multiple AI Tools for Layered Creativity

Dolores rarely relies on one AI tool. Instead, she chains tools together for deeper creativity.

Example workflow:

  1. Start with DALL·E 3 for initial concept generation.
  2. Use MidJourney for style refinement.
  3. Apply Stable Diffusion for texturing and detail enhancement.
  4. Finalize in Photoshop with AI-powered brushes.

Why this works: Each tool has unique strengths, and combining them reduces limitations.

Most AI tools use generalized datasets, but Dolores trains custom models to match her (or her clients’) unique aesthetic.

How to do it:

  • Step 1: Collect 10-20 high-quality images of your signature style.

Common Mistakes When Using AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the best artists make AI-related errors. Here are five pitfalls and how Dolores avoids them.

❌ Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI (Losing Your Signature Style)

Problem: Some artists let AI dictate their work, leading to generic, unoriginal designs. Solution: Always start with your own ideas, then use AI as a tool.

Dolores’ Fix:

"AI is a collaborator, not a replacement. If your art looks like it came from a stock library, you’re not using it right."

❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring Copyright & Ethical AI Use

Problem: Some AI tools train on copyrighted data, leading to legal risks. Solution: Use ethical AI datasets (e.g., CC0-licensed images) and check for plagiarism.

Dolores’ Fix:

"Always verify where your AI model was trained. If you’re unsure, fine-tune your own model instead."

❌ Mistake 3: Poor Prompting Leads to Low-Quality Output

Problem: Vague prompts = blurry, irrelevant, or low-detail images. Solution: Be specific, use modifiers, and test multiple versions.

Dolores’ Fix:

"If your AI output looks like a child drew it, your prompt is too weak. Add more details—style references, lighting, mood."

❌ Mistake 4: Not Backing Up AI-Generated Work

Problem: Some AI platforms change their policies, leading to lost work. Solution: Save every version in multiple formats (PNG, JPEG, PSD).

Dolores’ Fix:

"Treat AI art like a digital sketchbook—always export and archive your work."

❌ Mistake 5: Forgetting the Human Touch

Problem: Some AI art looks too perfect, too synthetic. Solution: Add imperfections (e.g., hand-drawn textures, subtle glitches).

Dolores’ Fix:

"The best AI art feels human. Don’t over-polish—leave room for artistic soul."
