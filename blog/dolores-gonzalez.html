1. Train a Custom AI Model on Your Artistic Style

Problem: Most AI art generators (like DALL·E or MidJourney) produce generic, cookie-cutter designs. If you want unique, recognizable work, you need a model tailored to your style.

Dolores’s Approach: AI Mosaic Studio developed "StyleDNA", a proprietary system that allows artists to train AI on their personal portfolio. By feeding the AI 100+ high-quality images of their work, it learns:

  • Color palettes
  • Brush strokes and textures
  • Composition preferences

How to Do It:

  • Collect a diverse sample of your best work (mix of styles if applicable).
  • Use platforms like Runway ML or Stable Diffusion’s "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) to fine-tune a model.
  • Iterate with feedback—ask AI to generate variations and refine based on what works.
