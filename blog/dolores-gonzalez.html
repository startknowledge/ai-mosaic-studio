Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Prompt Engineering Foundation

What it is: AI art quality depends 90% on the prompt. A well-crafted prompt guides the AI to generate the exact vision you want.

How Dolores González does it: She follows the "5C Framework" for prompts:

  1. Context – Set the scene (e.g., "A cyberpunk neon city at night").
  2. Concept – Define the theme (e.g., "steampunk meets futurism").
  3. Details – Specify key elements (e.g., "vintage mechanical robots, glowing holograms").
  4. Style – Choose an artistic direction (e.g., "Moebius-inspired, high contrast").
  5. Constraints – Limit variables for consistency (e.g., "only 3 primary colors: electric blue, magenta, gold").

Real-World Example: A client wanted a logo for a sustainable fashion brand. Instead of a vague prompt like "eco-friendly fashion logo," Dolores used: "A minimalist, hand-drawn logo featuring a recycled fabric texture, with a subtle watercolor wash effect, in organic greens and earthy browns. Style: mid-century modern with a handcrafted feel. Avoid clichés like leaves or trees."

Result: The AI generated 5 unique logo concepts in seconds, all aligning with the brand’s aesthetic.

Your Action Step:

  • Before generating art, write 3 different prompts for the same concept.
  • Test them and compare which one yields the best results.

  3. Human Refinement – Polishing the AI’s output to match her vision.
