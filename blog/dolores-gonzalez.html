Before diving into strategies, it’s essential to understand how Dolores González’s AI mosaics are created. Her process involves three key stages:

The final step is not purely digital—Dolores often manually adjusts tiles to add textural depth, ensuring that the AI-generated art feels tactile and real.

Common Mistakes When Experimenting with AI Mosaics (And How to Avoid Them)

While AI mosaics offer endless creative possibilities, there are pitfalls that even experienced artists can fall into. Here’s how to avoid them:

Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Input

Problem: Some artists let AI generate the entire piece without refinement, leading to cold, impersonal art. Solution:

  • Always review AI outputs critically—ask: "Does this feel human?"
  • Combine AI with traditional techniques (e.g., hand-painting over AI tiles).
  • Use AI as a collaborator, not a dictator.

Example of Failure: A digital artist used MidJourney to generate a mosaic, but the colors were clashing, and the composition felt robotic. After manually adjusting the palette, it became visually cohesive.

Mistake 2: Ignoring the Technical Limitations of AI

Problem: AI can’t perfectly replicate textures (e.g., real tile grouting, light reflections). Solution:

  • Use AI for concepts, not final textures.
  • Photoshop or manually enhance AI-generated mosaics for realism.
  • Test in 3D (using Blender or Unity) before finalizing.
