Common Mistakes When Using AI in Art (And How to Avoid Them)

While AI offers amazing creative possibilities, many artists make critical errors that limit their potential. Here are the most common mistakes—and how to prevent them—based on Dolores González’s insights.

1. Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Input

The Mistake: Some artists let AI generate the entire piece without any manual refinement, leading to generic, soulless art.

How to Avoid It:

  • Always treat AI as a tool, not the artist.
  • Edit, refine, and add personal touches to ensure the work feels authentic.

Example: A digital artist named David created an AI-generated portrait that looked perfect at first glance. However, when he removed all human edits, the piece lost its emotional depth, making it feel cold and mechanical.

2. Ignoring Copyright & Ethical AI Use

The Mistake: Some AI tools train on copyrighted material, leading to legal risks if you use them without proper licensing.

How to Avoid It:

  • Use AI tools that train on public domain or licensed datasets (e.g., AI-Mosaic’s ethical AI models).

The Mistake: Many artists use AI with default settings, leading to predictable, uninspired results.
