Chapter 3: Common Mistakes in AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most innovative AI artists make critical errors that can damage credibility or revenue. Here are the biggest pitfalls—and how González avoids them.

Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Touch

The Problem: Some artists let AI generate everything, leading to generic, soulless work.

González’s Fix:

  • AI should be a collaborator, not a replacement.
  • Always review, refine, and add human intent.

How to Avoid It: ✅ Use AI for 70% of the process, manual work for 30%. ✅ Ask: "Does this feel like me?" before finalizing.

Mistake 2: Ignoring Copyright & Ethical AI Training

The Problem: Some artists train AI on copyrighted art (e.g., famous paintings) without permission.

González’s Fix:

  • Only use your own work or public-domain images.
  • Avoid scraping artists’ work without consent.

How to Avoid It: ✅ Use AI-Mosaic’s "Ethical Training Dataset" (curated public-domain art). ✅ Credit original artists if using their style as inspiration.

Mistake 3: Not Testing AI Outputs with Real Audiences

The Problem: Artists assume AI-generated work will appeal to buyers—but audiences often reject it if it feels "too AI."

González’s Fix:

  • Run A/B tests with human vs. AI-generated versions.
