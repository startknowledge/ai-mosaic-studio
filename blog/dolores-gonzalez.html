In 2019, she founded AI Mosaic Studio, a creative agency specializing in:

Today, her studio works with clients ranging from indie artists to Fortune 500 companies, helping them integrate AI into their creative workflows without losing their unique artistic voice.

The Philosophy Behind AI Mosaic Studio: "AI as a Muse, Not a Replacement"

Dolores González firmly believes that AI should enhance, not replace, human creativity. Her studio’s approach is built on three core principles:

  1. Collaboration Over Competition

    • AI excels at pattern recognition, style transfer, and rapid iteration, but humans provide emotion, context, and narrative.
    • Example: She often uses AI to generate background textures that she then manually refines with watercolor brushstrokes, creating a seamless fusion of technology and tradition.

  2. Ethical AI Usage

    • Unlike some AI art platforms that rely on scraped copyrighted works, AI Mosaic Studio trains models on public domain art, custom datasets, and user-uploaded references with proper attribution.
    • She advocates for "AI with a conscience"—ensuring that generated art doesn’t infringe on existing intellectual property.

  3. Accessibility Without Sacrificing Quality

    • Many AI tools are expensive or overly complex for independent artists. Dolores has developed affordable, user-friendly AI workflows that even beginners can master.

Chapter 2: 8 Actionable Strategies from Dolores González to Master AI in Your Creative Workflow

Now that we understand her background, let’s explore practical, step-by-step strategies she uses to maximize AI in creative projects. Whether you're a graphic designer, illustrator, or marketer, these techniques will help you save time, enhance creativity, and produce high-impact work.

Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Prompt Engineering Foundation

Problem: Many artists struggle with AI tools because they don’t know how to communicate their vision effectively to the algorithm.

Dolores’s Solution: She treats prompt engineering as an art form, refining her inputs to get consistent, high-quality results.

How to Apply This:

  1. Be Specific, Not Vague

    • ❌ "Generate a futuristic city."
    • ✅ "A cyberpunk neon cityscape at dusk, inspired by Blade Runner 2049, with holographic billboards, rain-soaked streets, and a lone drone hovering over a skyscraper. Ultra HD, cinematic lighting, 8K resolution."

  2. Use Reference Images (When Allowed)

    • Upload a sketch, mood board, or existing artwork to guide the AI’s style.
    • Example: She once used a hand-drawn sketch of a dragon as a reference to generate AI-enhanced dragon illustrations with more depth and texture.

  3. Experiment with Style Blending

    • Combine two or more artistic styles in one prompt.
    • Example: "A portrait in the style of Van Gogh meets cyberpunk, with swirling brushstrokes and neon accents."

Real-World Example: A client wanted a custom logo for a tech startup. Instead of just saying "tech logo," Dolores crafted a prompt like: "A minimalist geometric logo for a quantum computing company, inspired by futuristic data streams, with a subtle holographic glow effect. Clean lines, monochromatic palette with a single accent color (electric blue). SVG format, vector-based."

The result? A logo that was 100% unique, scalable, and aligned with the brand’s vision—all generated in under 10 minutes.

Strategy 2: Use AI for Concept Development, Not Just Final Outputs

Problem: Many artists rely on AI only for final images, missing out on its brainstorming potential.

Dolores’s Solution: She uses AI early in the creative process to:

  • Generate multiple concept sketches
  • Test color palettes and compositions
  • Explore unconventional ideas

How to Apply This:

  1. AI as a "Thought Partner"

    • Use tools like MidJourney, DALL·E 3, or Stable Diffusion to generate dozens of variations of an idea before committing to one.
    • Example: If designing a book cover, she might ask: "Fantasy novel cover, medieval fantasy, dragon knight, dark academia, three different compositions."

  2. Refine with Human Input

    • Pick the most promising AI-generated concepts and manually refine them (e.g., redrawing outlines, adjusting colors).

Real-World Example: For a children’s book illustration, Dolores used AI to generate three different character designs before finalizing one. The AI suggested a whimsical, round-faced creature, which she then hand-painted with watercolors to add warmth and texture.

Strategy 3: Train Custom AI Models for Unique Styles

Problem: Generic AI models (like DALL·E) can’t replicate your personal artistic style perfectly.

Dolores’s Solution: She trains custom AI models using her own artwork to ensure consistency and brand identity.

How to Apply This:

  1. Gather a Diverse Dataset

    • Collect 100+ high-quality images of your best work (e.g., sketches, paintings, digital art).
    • Use platforms like Stable Diffusion’s "DreamBooth" or Runway ML to train a custom model.

  2. Fine-Tune for Specific Needs

    • Example: If you’re a character designer, train a model on your style of faces, outfits, and poses.
    • If you’re a brand designer, train it on your logo variations, typography, and color schemes.

  3. Iterate and Improve

    • Start with small batches, then expand as the model improves.

Real-World Example: A branding agency hired AI Mosaic Studio to create consistent AI-generated ads for their client. Instead of using a generic model, Dolores trained an AI on:

  • The client’s existing ad campaigns
  • Their brand’s color palette
  • Their typography style

The result? Ads that looked 100% on-brand, with zero manual tweaking needed for basic variations.

Strategy 4: Combine AI with Traditional Techniques for Hybrid Art

Problem: Pure AI art can sometimes feel too digital and impersonal.

Dolores’s Solution: She blends AI with traditional media (e.g., watercolors, ink, photography) to create unique hybrid works.

How to Apply This:

  1. Use AI for Backgrounds or Textures

    • Generate a noisy, abstract background with AI, then paint over it with acrylics or ink.
    • Example: She once used AI-generated cosmic textures as a base for a hand-painted space fantasy mural.

  2. AI-Assisted Sketching

    • Use AI to refine rough sketches before finalizing them.
    • Example: Upload a quick pencil sketch to an AI, ask it to "enhance the shading and details," then manually clean it up.

  3. Photoshop + AI Workflow

    • Generate an AI image, then manually add elements (e.g., hand-drawn characters, textures, or typography).

Real-World Example: For an art exhibition, Dolores created a series called "Digital Dreamscapes" where she:

  1. Generated AI landscapes in surreal styles.
  2. Printed them on canvas and hand-painted over them with oil paints.
  3. Added physical textures (e.g., glitter, metallic paint) for a tactile experience.

The result? Artworks that felt both digital and handcrafted, appealing to both tech-savvy and traditional art collectors.

Strategy 5: Automate Repetitive Tasks with AI Workflows

Problem: Artists often waste time on repetitive tasks (e.g., resizing images, generating thumbnails, creating social media posts).

Dolores’s Solution: She automates these tasks using AI, freeing up time for high-impact creativity.

How to Apply This:

  1. Batch-Generate Social Media Content

    • Use MidJourney or DALL·E to create multiple variations of a post in one go.
    • Example: For a YouTube channel, she generates 10 thumbnail concepts at once, then picks the best one.

  2. AI-Powered Image Resizing & Optimization

    • Tools like Adobe Firefly can automatically resize images for different platforms (Instagram, Twitter, Pinterest).
    • Example: She uploads a high-res AI-generated banner, and the tool auto-generates optimized versions for all social media.

  3. Automated Color Palette Extraction

    • Use AI tools like Coolors or Adobe Color to extract dominant colors from an image, then apply them to new designs.

Real-World Example: A freelance illustrator was struggling with consistent branding across platforms. Dolores helped her:

  1. Generate 10 AI character designs in one batch.
  2. Use AI color extraction to pull palettes from each design.
  3. Automatically resize and format them for Instagram, Twitter, and her website.

This saved her hours of manual work while keeping her brand cohesive.
