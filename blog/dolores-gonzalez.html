Common Mistakes When Using AI in Art (And How to Avoid Them)

While AI offers amazing opportunities, many creatives make critical errors that limit its potential. Here’s how to avoid the pitfalls:

Mistake 1: Relying Too Much on AI Without Human Input

Problem: Some artists submit AI-generated work as their own without refinement. Solution: AI should inspire, not replace human creativity.

How to Fix It:

  • Always add a personal touch—even if it’s just color adjustments or brushwork.
  • Dolores González’s Rule: "If an AI-generated piece looks identical to another artist’s work, it’s not original."

Mistake 2: Ignoring Copyright & Ethical AI Use

Problem: Some AI tools train on copyrighted data, leading to legal risks. Solution: Use ethical AI datasets and credit sources.

How to Fix It:

  • Stick to open-source AI models (e.g., Stable Diffusion’s official versions).
  • If using proprietary tools, check their terms of service for copyright policies.

Mistake 3: Overcomplicating the Process

Problem: Some creatives get stuck in AI experimentation without producing. Solution: Start small and scale up.

How to Fix It:

  • Begin with one AI tool (e.g., MidJourney for prompts, Photoshop for editing).
  • Dolores González’s Tip: "Master one AI technique before moving to the next."

Mistake 4: Not Testing AI-Generated Work with Real Audiences

Problem: Some artists assume AI-generated art will sell without validation. Solution: Gauge real reactions before full production.

How to Fix It:

  • Post AI-assisted work on social media and ask for feedback.
  • Use AI Mosaic’s "Audience Predictor" to simulate how different styles will perform.

Mistake 5: Underpricing AI-Assisted Work

Problem: Some creatives charge less for AI help than for manual work. Solution: AI speeds up production, but human skill adds value.

How to Fix It:

  • Bundle AI drafts + human refinement as a premium service.
  • Dolores González’s Pricing Strategy: "If AI does 60% of the work, charge 30% less—but offer a 'Deluxe' option for full customization."

Mistake 6: Not Keeping Up with AI Advancements

Problem: AI evolves rapidly, and outdated tools can limit creativity. Solution: Stay updated with new AI art technologies.

How to Fix It:

  • Follow AI Mosaic’s blog and Dolores González’s LinkedIn for updates.
