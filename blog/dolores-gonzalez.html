8 Actionable Strategies to Master AI Art Like Dolores González

Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Prompt Engineering Foundation

Prompt engineering is the art of crafting detailed, structured inputs for AI tools to produce the best possible results. González emphasizes that 90% of AI art success depends on the prompt.

How to Write High-Converting AI Prompts (Like a Pro)

  1. Be Specific with Details

    • ❌ "A fantasy dragon"
    • ✅ "A cyberpunk dragon with neon-blue scales, glowing red eyes, and a metallic chest plate, standing on a floating island in a rainstorm, cinematic lighting, ultra-detailed, 8K"

  2. Use Style References

    • Mention artists or styles you admire:
      • "In the style of Zdzisław Beksiński meets H.R. Giger, hyper-detailed, 3D-rendered"

  3. Incorporate Mood and Atmosphere

    • AI responds better to emotional cues:
      • "A melancholic cyberpunk city at night, with rain-soaked streets and flickering neon signs, cinematic depth of field"

  4. Experiment with Negative Prompts

    • Tell the AI what not to include:
      • "A beautiful fantasy forest, but avoid any human figures, keep it mystical and eerie"

Real-World Example: One of González’s students, Maria L., a freelance illustrator, struggled with consistent character designs. By refining her prompts to include: "A cyberpunk samurai with a high-tech katana, cybernetic arm, glowing red aura, ultra-detailed, 8K, inspired by Blade Runner 2049 and Studio Ghibli" she doubled her client requests within three months.

Strategy 2: Use AI for Concept Development, Not Just Final Outputs

González teaches that AI should be a tool for brainstorming, not the sole creator of your art. Here’s how to leverage it effectively:

  1. Generate Multiple Concepts Quickly

    • Instead of staring at a blank canvas, use AI to create 5–10 variations of an idea.
    • Example: If designing a logo for a tech startup, prompt: "5 futuristic logo concepts for a blockchain company, minimalist, geometric, with a subtle cyberpunk twist, high contrast, vector-friendly"

  2. Refine AI Outputs with Manual Touches

    • Take the best AI-generated elements (e.g., a character’s face, a background texture) and combine them with hand-drawn details.
    • Example: González often uses AI for the background in her illustrations but draws the main subject by hand to add a personal touch.

  3. Create AI-Assisted Mood Boards

    • Use tools like MidJourney or DALL·E to generate 5–10 visual references for a project before starting.
    • Example: For a fantasy novel cover, she might generate:
      • "A medieval knight with a glowing sword, standing on a cliff overlooking a stormy sea, dark fantasy, inspired by Alan Lee"
      • "A sorceress with floating orbs of light, ancient ruins in the background, mystical, ultra-detailed"

Common Mistake: Relying entirely on AI for final art without any human input, leading to generic, unoriginal work.
