8 Actionable Strategies to Apply AI in Your Artistic Projects

If you’re an artist, designer, or creative professional looking to integrate AI into your workflow, Dolores González’s methods offer practical, step-by-step approaches. Here are eight proven strategies to get started:

1. Start with AI-Assisted Sketching

Problem: Many artists struggle with blocking out ideas before diving into detailed work. Solution: Use AI sketching tools (like MidJourney or DALL·E) to generate initial concepts and refine them manually.

How to Apply:

  • Input a rough idea (e.g., "a futuristic cityscape with neon lights").
  • Let AI generate multiple variations.
  • Select the best base sketch and hand-draw over it for refinement.

Example: A graphic designer used AI to generate logo concepts for a client, then manually adjusted the typography and colors to match brand guidelines.

2. Use AI for Color Palette Generation

Problem: Choosing harmonious color schemes can be time-consuming. Solution: AI tools like Adobe Color or Dolphin can analyze images and suggest palettes based on mood, season, or trend.

How to Apply:

  • Upload a reference image (e.g., a landscape or fashion design).
  • Let AI generate 5-10 color schemes.
  • Test them in mockups before finalizing.

Example: A fashion illustrator used AI to match a client’s brand colors to a new collection, saving hours of trial and error.

3. Generate AI-Backed Backgrounds & Textures

Problem: Creating unique textures (like fabric, wood, or digital noise) can be tedious. Solution: Use AI texture generators (e.g., TextureTools or AI Texture) to produce high-quality backgrounds in seconds.

How to Apply:

  • Describe the texture you need (e.g., "grungy concrete wall").
  • Let AI generate multiple options.
  • Composite them into Photoshop or Procreate for final touches.

Example: A concept artist used AI-generated ruin textures to enhance a cyberpunk cityscape, giving it a more immersive feel.

4. Automate Repetitive Design Tasks with AI

Problem: Tasks like resizing images, batch-editing, or generating thumbnails waste time. Solution: AI tools like Runway ML or Topaz Video AI can automate repetitive workflows.

How to Apply:

  • Use Runway ML’s "Inpaint" tool to remove objects from photos.
  • Apply AI upscaling to enhance low-res images without losing quality.
  • Generate social media thumbnails in bulk using AI templates.

Example: A digital marketer used AI to auto-generate Instagram posts for a client, saving 10+ hours per week.

5. Experiment with AI-Powered Style Transfer

Problem: Artists want to mix different artistic styles but lack the technical skills. Solution: Neural style transfer tools (like DeepDream or AI Image Generator) can apply famous art styles to your work.

How to Apply:

  • Upload a base image (e.g., a portrait).
  • Choose a style (e.g., Van Gogh’s brushstrokes or Hokusai’s woodblock prints).
  • Adjust blend ratios for a custom hybrid look.

Example: A portrait artist used AI to add a "watercolor effect" to client photos, creating a unique digital painting style.

6. Use AI for Generative Art & Procedural Design

Problem: Creating infinite variations of a design (e.g., for logos, patterns, or game assets) is labor-intensive. Solution: Generative AI tools (like Generative Design by Autodesk) can produce endless unique designs based on rules.

How to Apply:

  • Define parameters (e.g., "a geometric pattern with 3-5 colors").
  • Let AI generate 100+ variations.
  • Select the best options and refine them manually.

Example: A game developer used AI to create procedural terrain textures, reducing the time needed for world-building by 70%.

7. Collaborate with AI for Storytelling & Concept Art

Problem: Developing narrative-driven art (e.g., for books, films, or games) requires strong conceptual skills. Solution: AI can generate story prompts, character designs, and world maps to spark creativity.

How to Apply:

  • Use AI writing tools (like Jasper or Sudowrite) to generate plot ideas.
  • Input character descriptions (e.g., "a cybernetic samurai in a dystopian city") and let AI sketch initial concepts.
  • Refine the storyboard with AI-assisted illustration.

Example: A children’s book illustrator used AI to develop character backstories, leading to more engaging and cohesive illustrations.

8. Optimize AI Workflows for Speed & Efficiency

Problem: AI tools can be overwhelming if not used strategically. Solution: Streamline your AI workflow by:

  • Batch-processing multiple images at once.
  • Using AI shortcuts (e.g., DALL·E’s "improve" feature).
  • Integrating AI with existing software (e.g., Photoshop + MidJourney).

How to Apply:

  • Pre-set AI prompts for common tasks (e.g., "portrait of a futuristic warrior").
  • Use AI to auto-correct color grading in Adobe Lightroom.
  • Automate social media content with AI-generated captions and hashtags.

Example: A freelance illustrator set up an AI-powered workflow where DALL·E generated thumbnails, Canva auto-formatted them, and Buffer scheduled posts, tripling her productivity.
