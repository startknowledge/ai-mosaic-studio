8 Actionable Strategies to Master AI Art (Inspired by Dolores González)

Now that we understand her philosophy and impact, let’s dive into practical strategies to create with AI like a pro.

Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Concept Before Using AI

Problem: Many artists jump straight into AI tools without a clear vision, leading to generic, uninspired outputs.

Dolores’ Approach:

  • Define your artistic intent before generating anything.
  • Ask yourself:
    • What emotion do I want to evoke?
    • What story does this piece tell?
    • What unique elements should remain human-made?

Real-World Example: A client wanted AI-generated illustrations for a children’s book. Instead of letting the AI freeform, Dolores sketched a rough storyboard, then used AI to refine character designs and backgrounds while keeping the core narrative intact.

How to Apply This:

  1. Sketch or describe your idea in detail.
  2. Use AI as a collaborator—not a replacement.
  3. Iterate until the AI output aligns with your vision.

Strategy 2: Use AI for Concept Exploration, Not Final Outputs

Problem: Relying solely on AI for final artworks can lead to lack of originality.

Dolores’ Approach:

  • AI is a brainstorming tool, not a finisher.
  • Example: She uses AI to generate 100+ variations of a design, then picks the best elements to combine manually.

Real-World Example: For a brand identity project, Dolores fed AI keywords like "futuristic," "organic," and "minimalist" to generate logo concepts. She then hand-edited the best ones, adding custom textures and typography to make them unique.

How to Apply This:

  • Generate multiple AI drafts (50-100).
  • Select the strongest elements from each.
  • Combine and refine manually.

Strategy 3: Train AI on Your Personal Style

Problem: Generic AI models produce predictable results because they’re trained on broad datasets.

Dolores’ Approach:

  • Fine-tune AI models with your personal artwork to match your style.
  • Example: She uses Stable Diffusion’s "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) to train AI on her mosaic techniques, ensuring outputs align with her aesthetic.

Real-World Example: A graphic designer wanted AI to mimic her hand-drawn illustrations. Dolores helped her upload 50 of her best pieces to train an AI model, resulting in AI-generated art that felt "her" rather than generic.

How to Apply This:

  1. Collect 30-50 of your best works (high-resolution).
  2. Use AI training tools (like MidJourney’s "Custom Models" or Stable Diffusion’s LoRA).
  3. Test and refine until the AI matches your style.

Strategy 4: Combine AI with Traditional Techniques

Problem: Pure AI art can feel cold and impersonal.

Dolores’ Approach:

  • Blend AI with traditional methods (e.g., hand-painting over AI backgrounds).
  • Example: She uses AI to generate a complex pattern, then applies it to a physical mosaic for a hybrid digital-physical artwork.

Real-World Example: An artist wanted to create AI-assisted murals. Dolores suggested:

How to Apply This:

  • Keep human touch for key details (faces, textures, storytelling).

Common Mistakes in AI Art (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most talented artists make AI-related pitfalls. Here’s how to steer clear of them:

Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Input

❌ Problem: Letting AI do everything leads to generic, uninspired art. ✅ Solution:

  • Use AI as a tool, not a replacement.
  • Always add your personal touch (even if it’s just color correction or fine details).

Mistake 2: Ignoring Copyright and AI Training Data

❌ Problem: Some AI models are trained on copyrighted art, leading to legal risks. ✅ Solution:

  • Use open-source AI models (e.g., Stable Diffusion, MidJourney’s fair-use datasets).
  • Avoid training AI on others’ work without permission.

Mistake 3: Not Testing AI Outputs Before Finalizing

❌ Problem: Assuming AI will always produce high-quality results is a mistake. ✅ Solution:

  • Always review AI outputs before using them.
  • Have a backup plan (e.g., manual redraws if needed).

Mistake 4: Underestimating the Learning Curve

❌ Problem: AI tools can be overwhelming for beginners. ✅ Solution:

  • Start with simple prompts (e.g., "a futuristic city").
  • Gradually experiment with more complex requests.
  • Join AI art communities (e.g., Discord groups, Reddit’s r/AIArt).

Mistake 5: Not Keeping Up with AI Advancements

❌ Problem: AI evolves rapidly, and old methods can become obsolete. ✅ Solution:

  • Follow AI art blogs (e.g., AI Art News, Creative Bloq).
  • Experiment with new tools (e.g., Sora, Google’s Imagen 2).
  • Attend AI art conferences (e.g., Neural Conference, SIGGRAPH).
