8 Actionable Strategies to Master AI Art Like Dolores Gonzalez

Dolores Gonzalez’s methods aren’t just theoretical—they’re practical, step-by-step approaches any artist can implement. Below are eight proven strategies to elevate your AI art game.

Strategy 1: Start with a Strong Concept Before Using AI

Problem: Many artists jump straight into AI tools without a clear vision, leading to generic, uninspired outputs.

Dolores’ Solution: Before running an AI prompt, ask yourself:

  • What emotion or message do I want to convey?
  • What are the key visual elements (colors, shapes, textures)?
  • How can AI enhance, not replace, my original idea?

Example: A digital illustrator wanted to create a "cyberpunk dystopia" scene. Instead of just typing "cyberpunk city at night," she broke it down:

  • Mood: "Dark, neon-lit, oppressive, with a sense of decay."
  • Key Elements: "Futuristic skyscrapers, holographic billboards, rain-soaked streets, a lone figure in a trench coat."
  • AI Role: Use AI to generate background textures and enhance lighting effects, while she painted the main character manually.

Result: A visually striking, emotionally rich piece that stood out in a competitive digital art market.

Action Step: Always sketch or brainstorm before using AI. Use tools like Miro or Notion to map out your concept.

Strategy 2: Use AI for "Pre-Visualization" Before Finalizing Art

Problem: Artists often spend hours refining a piece only to realize it doesn’t align with their vision.

Dolores’ Solution: Leverage AI for quick, low-commitment pre-visualizations to test ideas before full production.

Example: A graphic designer was working on a brand identity for a tech startup. Instead of spending weeks designing logos, she:

  1. Typed prompts like "minimalist tech logo, futuristic, geometric, corporate, blue and white palette."
  2. Generated 20 variations in seconds using MidJourney.
  3. Selected the strongest concepts and refined them manually.

Result: Saved 10+ hours of design time while ensuring the final logo was on-brand and impactful.

Action Step:

  • Use DALL·E 3 or Stable Diffusion for rapid logo/mood board generation.
  • Export 3-5 top options and refine them in Photoshop or Procreate.

Strategy 3: Combine AI with Traditional Techniques for Unique Styles

Problem: AI-generated art can sometimes feel too uniform or "machine-like."

Dolores’ Solution: Blend AI-generated elements with hand-painted or traditional techniques for distinctive, human-like art.

Example: A street artist wanted to create a "digital graffiti mural" but didn’t want it to look entirely AI-made. She:

  1. Used AI to generate a rough graffiti-style background (prompt: "street graffiti, neon colors, urban, 3D effect").
  2. Hand-painted over it with acrylic splatters and textures to add organic imperfections.
  3. Added a human element—a sketch of a person in the foreground, drawn traditionally.

Result: A hybrid piece that felt both digital and tactile, gaining attention on Instagram and ArtStation.

Action Step:

  • Try AI for backgrounds/textures + hand-drawn elements.
  • Experiment with AI-generated color palettes but apply them manually.

Strategy 4: Train Your Own AI Model for Personalized Art Styles

Problem: Generic AI models (like MidJourney) can’t fully capture an artist’s unique style.

Dolores’ Solution: Use fine-tuning techniques to train AI on your personal artwork, creating a custom style generator.

Example: A painter wanted AI to mimic her impressionist brushstrokes. She:

  1. Collected 50 of her best paintings and uploaded them to Runway ML.
  2. Trained a custom Stable Diffusion model using her style as a reference.
  3. Generated new pieces that looked unmistakably hers, but with AI efficiency.

Result: She could now create variations of her work in minutes, expanding her portfolio without losing authenticity.

Action Step:

  • Use Stable Diffusion’s "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) to fine-tune AI on your art.
  • Platforms like Civic AI offer style transfer tools for non-coders.

Strategy 5: Automate Repetitive Tasks with AI Workflows

Problem: Artists waste time on menial tasks like resizing, color correction, or background removal.

Dolores’ Solution: Set up AI-powered automation to handle repetitive work, freeing up mental energy for creativity.

Example: A digital illustrator was exhausted from manually removing backgrounds from her sketches. She:

  1. Used Adobe Firefly’s "Remove Background" tool to auto-clean up her line art.
  2. Set up a Zapier automation to auto-resize and export files for social media.
  3. Used AI to generate alt text for her posts (via Copy.ai), improving SEO.

Result: Saved 5+ hours per week, allowing her to focus on new creative projects.

Action Step:

  • Batch-process tasks with BatchOCR or AutoDesk’s AI tools.
  • Automate social media with Canva + AI captions.

Strategy 6: Use AI for "What-If" Scenarios in Storytelling

Problem: Writers and artists struggle to visualize complex scenes before committing to them.

Dolores’ Solution: Generate AI-rendered concept art to test storytelling ideas before full production.

Strategy 7: Monetize AI-Generated Art with Smart Licensing

Problem: Many artists don’t know how to legally sell AI art, leading to copyright issues.

Dolores’ Solution: Use AI Mosaic Studio’s "Creative Commons + Custom Licensing" model to protect and profit from AI art.

Example: A digital artist sold AI-generated NFTs but faced backlash over "AI ownership." Dolores advised:

  1. Disclose AI usage clearly in descriptions (e.g., "Generated with MidJourney, final touches by [Artist]").
  2. Offer multiple licensing tiers:
    • Free (CC0): For personal use.
    • Commercial (Paid): For businesses (with attribution).
    • Exclusive (High-Tier): For brands wanting full rights.
  3. Use smart contracts (via Rarible or OpenSea) to automate royalties.

Result: She tripled her NFT sales while avoiding legal disputes.

Action Step:

  • Check platform terms (e.g., Etsy allows AI art if disclosed).
  • Use watermarks for unsold drafts to prevent theft.

Strategy 8: Build a Community Around AI Art

Problem: Many artists feel isolated in the AI art space, missing out on collaboration and feedback.

Dolores’ Solution: Host AI art challenges, workshops, and Discord groups to foster creativity and networking.

  1. Received a prompt (e.g., "Generate a surreal landscape using AI, then add hand-painted details").
