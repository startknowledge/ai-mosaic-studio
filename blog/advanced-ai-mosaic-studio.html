Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

** ### **

Mistake 1: Skipping the Input Image Optimization

** ❌ **Problem:** Using **low-quality images** leads to **pixelated mosaics**. ✅ **Solution:** - **Resize images to 2000x2000px minimum**. - **Use AI upscaling** (Topaz, Let’s Enhance) before mosaic generation. ### **

Mistake 2: Overcomplicating Tile Selection

** ❌ **Problem:** Mixing **too many tile types** (e.g., faces + abstract shapes) creates **visual chaos**. ✅ **Solution:** - **Stick to 1-2 dominant tile styles** (e.g., **all portraits or all geometric shapes**). ### **

Mistake 3: Ignoring Color Harmony

** ❌ **Problem:** **Clashing colors** in tiles make the mosaic look **unprofessional**. ✅ **Solution:** - Use **AI color matching** (e.g., **Adobe Color, Coolors**). - **Test mosaics in grayscale** first to check **contrast balance**. ### **

Mistake 4: Not Experimenting with AI Styles

** ❌ **Problem:** Sticking to **realistic tiles** limits creativity. ✅ **Solution:** - Try **surreal, cyberpunk, or minimalist AI styles**. - Use **MidJourney/DALL·E 3** to generate **unique tile sets**. ### **

Mistake 5: Forgetting SEO & Accessibility

** ❌ **Problem:** **Large mosaic files** slow down websites. ✅ **Solution:** - **Compress images** (TinyPNG, Squoosh). - **Add alt text** for **SEO & accessibility** (e.g., *"AI-generated mosaic of Paris landmarks"*). ### **

Mistake 6: Not Backing Up AI-Generated Work

** ❌ **Problem:** **AI tools can glitch**, and **unsaved mosaics are lost**. ✅ **Solution:** - **Save multiple versions** (AI Mosaic Studio’s **auto-save feature**). - **Use cloud storage** (Google Drive, Dropbox) for **backup**. --- ## **
