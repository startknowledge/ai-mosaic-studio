Implementing Mosaic™ effectively requires more than just plugging in data. To unlock its full potential, businesses must adopt structured strategies that align technology with business objectives. Here are eight proven approaches:

  • ERP systems (SAP, Oracle)

Problem: Even the best AI tool is useless if users don’t trust or understand it. Solution: Invest in training and change management:

Problem: AI can be overconfident in wrong answers (a phenomenon called "AI hubris"). Solution: Use human-in-the-loop validation—always have domain experts review AI suggestions.

Example: A supply chain manager using Mosaic™ for vendor risk assessment might:

  1. Get AI-generated risk scores for 50 suppliers.

Action Step: ✔ Design workflows where AI provides suggestions, but humans approve final decisions. ✔ Use Mosaic™’s explainability features to understand AI logic. ✔ Document human-AI collaboration for audit trails.

  • Track AI accuracy (e.g., "Did the recommendation lead to the expected outcome?").
  • Update training data with new market conditions.
  • Retrain the model every 3-6 months (or after major business changes).
