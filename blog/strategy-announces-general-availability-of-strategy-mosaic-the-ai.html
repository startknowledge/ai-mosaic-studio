Strategy 4: Leverage AI-Generated Insights (But Validate Them)

Problem: AI can hallucinate—meaning it may generate false or misleading insights if not properly validated.

Solution: Mosaic™ provides AI-driven recommendations, but human oversight is critical. Follow this validation framework:

  1. Cross-Check with Domain Experts – Does the insight align with industry knowledge?
  2. Test with Historical Data – Does the prediction hold up when compared to past trends?
  3. A/B Test Recommendations – For example, if AI suggests a price increase, run a limited test before full rollout.
  4. Set Up Alerts for Anomalies – Mosaic™ can flag unusual patterns that may need manual review.

Example: A luxury watch retailer used Mosaic™ to predict customer lifetime value (CLV). The AI suggested that customers who bought a $5,000 watch were 3x more likely to upgrade to a $20,000 model. However, upon manual review, the team found that these customers were actually high-net-worth individuals who purchased multiple watches at once—not necessarily planning an upgrade. The AI’s prediction was correct in trend but misleading in actionable insight.

Action Step: Implement a "Red Team" approach—have a skeptical analyst challenge AI recommendations before implementation.
