Even the most forward-thinking companies make critical AI implementation mistakes. Here are the biggest pitfalls and how to avoid them.

Mistake 1: Skipping the Data Cleanup Phase

Problem: AI models only work as well as the data they’re trained on. Dirty, incomplete, or inconsistent data leads to poor results. Solution:

  • Audit your data for duplicates, errors, and missing values.
  • Use Mosaic™’s built-in data cleaning tools to standardize formats.
  • Partner with data scientists to improve data quality.

Example: A company tried to predict customer churn but had incomplete customer history. Their AI model performed poorly, leading to false churn predictions.

Mistake 2: Overlooking Change Management

Problem: Employees resist AI adoption if they don’t understand how it benefits them. Solution:

  • Train staff on how to use Mosaic™ effectively.
  • Highlight time savings (e.g., "This AI will handle 80% of your reporting").
  • Gather feedback and iterate based on user needs.

Example: A company rolled out AI without training—employees feared job loss and didn’t use the tool. After workshops and clear communication, adoption rose by 70%.
