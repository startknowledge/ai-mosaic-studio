  • Use Mosaic™’s explainability features to understand AI logic.

❌ Mistake 2: Overloading AI with Poor Data

Problem: Garbage in, garbage out—low-quality data leads to bad AI insights. Solution:

  • Clean and structure data before feeding it to AI.
  • Use Mosaic™’s data validation tools to ensure accuracy.

❌ Mistake 3: Ignoring Ethical AI Usage

Problem: AI can reinforce biases if not monitored. Solution:

❌ Mistake 4: Not Customizing AI for Your Workflow

❌ Mistake 6: Forgetting to Monitor AI Performance
