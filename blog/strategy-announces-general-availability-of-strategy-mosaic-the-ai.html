Mistake: Treating AI as a standalone tool instead of seamlessly embedding it into workflows. Solution:

  • Connect to ERP (SAP, Oracle), CRM (Salesforce), and data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery).
  • Use APIs to trigger actions (e.g., Mosaic™ suggests a price change → ERP updates inventory).
  • Ensure real-time data sync to avoid stale insights.

  • Audit AI recommendations to ensure accuracy and fairness.
