Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcrowding: Avoid including too many photos in one collage. This can make it look cluttered and overwhelming.
  • Ignoring Aspect Ratio: Pay attention to the aspect ratio of your photos to ensure they fit well within the collage layout.
  • Poor Quality Photos: Using low-resolution or blurry photos can ruin the overall look of your collage.
  • Inconsistent Style: Stick to a consistent style and theme to create a cohesive look.
  • Neglecting Editing: Always review and edit your collage before finalizing it to catch any mistakes or areas for improvement.
