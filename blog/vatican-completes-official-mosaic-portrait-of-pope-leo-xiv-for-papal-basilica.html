Common Mistakes in Mosaic Restoration & How to Avoid Them

Even the most well-intentioned restorers can make costly errors. Here are five pitfalls and how to prevent them:

1. Using Modern Materials Instead of Historical Ones

Mistake: Replacing original glass tesserae with cheaper modern alternatives. Solution: Always source materials from the same era (e.g., Venetian glass for Byzantine mosaics).

2. Over-Cleaning with Harsh Chemicals

Mistake: Using bleach or abrasive pads that strip away original pigments. Solution: Test cleaning methods on small, hidden areas first.

3. Ignoring Structural Damage Before Restoration

Mistake: Focusing only on visual repairs without fixing cracks or loose tesserae. Solution: Assess structural integrity before any aesthetic work.

4. Skipping Documentation

Mistake: Not recording the restoration process, leading to lost knowledge. Solution: Photograph, videograph, and log every step.

5. Rushing the Process for Cost Savings

Mistake: Cutting corners to speed up restoration, leading to poor quality work. Solution: Prioritize quality over speed—mosaics are long-term investments.
