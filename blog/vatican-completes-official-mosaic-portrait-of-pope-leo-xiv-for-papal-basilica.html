Common Mistakes in Mosaic Restoration & How to Avoid Them

Even with AI assistance, human errors can still occur. Here are five critical mistakes and how to prevent them:

1. Over-Restoring: Adding Unnecessary Details

Problem: Some restorers invent details that weren’t originally there, altering the artwork’s authenticity. Solution:

  • Follow historical documentation (if available)
  • Use AI to highlight only missing sections
  • Consult multiple experts before making changes

2. Using Modern Pigments on Ancient Surfaces

Problem: New pigments may react poorly with old materials, causing fading or discoloration. Solution:

  • Research original pigment compositions (using X-ray fluorescence)
  • Test compatible modern alternatives in small areas first

3. Neglecting Structural Stability

Problem: Weakened mortar or improper support can lead to collapses. Solution:

  • Use 3D scanning to assess structural integrity
  • Reinforce with modern composite materials when needed

4. Skipping Digital Documentation

Problem: Without digital records, future restorations lose context and accuracy. Solution:

  • Scan every step of the restoration process
  • Store data in interoperable formats (e.g., OBJ, STL)

5. Ignoring Environmental Factors

Problem: Pollution, humidity, and temperature fluctuations accelerate decay. Solution:

  • Install climate control systems
  • Use anti-pollution filters in display areas
  • Conduct regular AI-based condition assessments
