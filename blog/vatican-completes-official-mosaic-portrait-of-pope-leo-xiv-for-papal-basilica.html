  • Complex, symbolic compositions (e.g., the Apse Mosaic of St. Peter’s Basilica)
  • A medium for theological teaching (e.g., the Last Judgment scenes)

The new mosaic of Pope Leo XIV follows this tradition, but with a modern twist—incorporating AI-assisted design to ensure historical accuracy while adapting to contemporary conservation needs.
