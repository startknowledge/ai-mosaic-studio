What is a Wedding Photo Mosaic?

A wedding photo mosaic is a collage of smaller images that come together to form a larger, more intricate picture. Each small image is a piece of the puzzle, contributing to the overall design. Common Mistakes to Avoid

While creating a wedding photo mosaic can be a fun and rewarding experience, there are some common mistakes to avoid:

  • Using Low-Quality Photos: Low-quality photos can result in a blurry or pixelated mosaic.
  • Overcrowding the Mosaic: Too many small images can make the mosaic look cluttered and lose its impact.
  • Ignoring the Main Image: The main image should be the focal point, so choose one that stands out.
  • Not Previewing Before Finalizing: Always preview your mosaic before finalizing to ensure it meets your expectations.
  • Forgetting to Customize: Customization options are there to make your mosaic unique, so don’t forget to take advantage of them.
