Introduction to MicroLEDs and Microsoft's Innovation

MicroLEDs, or micro-light emitting diodes, are a type of display technology that uses an array of microscopic LEDs to produce images. While they have traditionally been used in high-end consumer electronics, Microsoft has been exploring their potential in data center design. By using inexpensive MicroLEDs, Microsoft aims to reduce the energy consumption and increase the efficiency of their data centers. This innovation has the potential to revolutionize the way data centers are designed and operated, making them more sustainable and cost-effective.
