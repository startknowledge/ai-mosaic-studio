Q1: What is the difference between Automation and AI?

A1: Automation refers to the use of technical tools to perform tasks that have been assigned to humans. AI is the empowerment of machines to take action on behalf of humans, using their unique decision-making capabilities.

Q2: Can AI truly replace human intuition?

A2: While AI has the potential to emulate human intuition, it is not capable of replacing human creativity or intuition in strategic decision-making.

Q3: How do AI models learn from data?

A3: AI models learn from data through a process called machine learning, where algorithms are trained on a dataset to make predictions, classify patterns, or make decisions.

Q4: What are the challenges of integrating AI into existing workflows?

A4: The primary challenges of integrating AI into existing workflows include ensuring data quality, building trust in AI-powered recommendations, and integrating AI systems with legacy systems.

Q5: What role does human judgment play in AI decision-making?

A5: Human judgment plays a crucial role in AI decision-making, ensuring that AI-powered recommendations are aligned with human values and standards, and are free from biases.
