Chapter 1: The Evolution of AI in Visual Effects – From Traditional Pipelines to AI-Driven Innovation

1.1 The Traditional VFX Pipeline: Time-Consuming and Cost-Prohibitive

Before AI, VFX production was a labor-intensive, multi-stage process that required:

  • Pre-production: Concept art, storyboarding, and shot breakdowns
  • Production: Live-action filming, motion capture, and green screen shoots
  • Post-production: Rotoscoping, compositing, 3D modeling, and rendering

Each stage could take months to years, with costs ranging from $500,000 to $50 million+ for blockbuster films (e.g., "Avatar" cost $237 million in VFX alone).

1.2 The AI Revolution: How Machine Learning is Changing the Game

AI has accelerated VFX workflows by:

✅ Automating repetitive tasks (e.g., background removal, object tracking) ✅ Generating realistic assets (e.g., AI-generated characters, environments) ✅ Enabling deepfake and AI upscaling (e.g., restoring old footage, enhancing resolution) ✅ Reducing costs (e.g., AI can replace expensive motion capture suits)

Mosaic VFX harnesses these advancements to deliver high-end VFX at a fraction of the time and cost of traditional methods.

1.3 Why "Built from the Flood Up"?

The phrase "built from the flood up" suggests starting anew with modern, AI-driven techniques rather than relying on outdated methods. For Mosaic VFX, this means:

  • No reliance on expensive hardware (AI runs on cloud-based neural networks)
  • No need for massive VFX teams (AI handles most of the heavy lifting)
  • Instant scalability (projects can be adjusted in real-time without delays)

This approach is ideal for indie filmmakers, advertisers, and brands looking for cost-effective, high-impact visuals.

If you’re looking to leverage AI VFX like Mosaic does, here are eight proven strategies to implement in your workflow:

Strategy 1: Use AI for Background Removal & Compositing

Problem: Traditional green screen compositing is time-consuming and requires precise lighting.

AI Solution:

  • Tools like Remove.bg, Adobe Sensei, or Mosaic VFX’s proprietary AI can automatically separate subjects from backgrounds in seconds.
