Common Mistakes When Using AI VFX & How to Avoid Them

While AI VFX offers unprecedented efficiency, misuse can lead to unnatural results or ethical concerns. Here are five common pitfalls and how to prevent them.

Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on AI Without Human Oversight

Problem: AI can generate visually plausible but artistically weak content if not guided properly.

Solution:

  • Use AI as a starting point, then refine with human creativity.
  • Train AI models on your specific artistic style (e.g., if you prefer cyberpunk aesthetics, feed it examples of Blade Runner or Cyberpunk 2077).
  • Always review AI outputs for logical inconsistencies (e.g., a character’s shadow not matching their movement).

Example: A game studio used AI to generate entire levels for their game. While the environments looked visually correct, they lacked cohesive world-building. By manually adjusting key assets, they maintained narrative consistency.

Mistake 2: Ignoring Ethical & Legal Concerns with Deepfakes

Problem: AI-generated deepfakes can violate privacy, mislead audiences, or spread misinformation.

Solution:

  • Only use AI for creative purposes (e.g., fictional characters, historical reenactments).
  • Disclose AI-generated content (e.g., add a watermark or disclaimer).
  • Avoid creating deepfakes of real people without explicit consent.

Example: A news outlet used AI to recreate a historical speech for a documentary. They consulted ethicists to ensure no real individuals were misrepresented, and clearly labeled the content as AI-generated.

Mistake 3: Poor Integration with Existing Workflows

Problem: Some AI tools don’t play well with traditional VFX pipelines (e.g., After Effects, Maya, Unreal Engine).

Solution:

  • Choose AI tools with industry-standard plugins (e.g., Mosaic VFX’s Unreal Engine integration).
  • Test AI outputs in your final render engine before full production.
  • Use AI for repetitive tasks (e.g., background removal, texture generation) while keeping key artistic decisions manual.

Example: A VFX supervisor at a major studio tested Mosaic VFX’s AI character animation but found that some movements looked unnatural in final renders. By fine-tuning the AI model with their team’s style guide, they achieved seamless integration.

Mistake 4: Neglecting Quality Control in Bulk AI Generation

Problem: AI can generate inconsistencies (e.g., different textures, lighting variations) when used for large-scale projects.

Solution:

  • Apply AI in batches and manually inspect each segment.
  • Use AI as a "first draft" and refine manually for key scenes.
  • Set quality thresholds (e.g., "Only accept AI-generated textures with 90%+ accuracy").

Example: A film studio used AI to generate 100+ props for a fantasy movie. While most were high-quality, a few had unrealistic reflections. By applying a manual polish pass, they maintained cinematic standards.

Mistake 5: Underestimating the Cost of AI Training & Maintenance

Problem: Some AI models require expensive GPUs, cloud computing, or ongoing training.

Solution:

  • Start with cloud-based AI tools (e.g., Mosaic VFX’s subscription model) before investing in in-house AI infrastructure.
  • Leverage pre-trained models (e.g., Stable Diffusion, NVIDIA Omniverse) to reduce training costs.
  • Monitor AI performance and update models as needed to maintain accuracy.
