Technology Stack

AI Mosaic Studio is built using modern web technologies to deliver a fast, secure, and feature-rich experience entirely within your browser.

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
  • AI & Computer Vision: MediaPipe (face and pose detection)
  • Image Processing: HTML5 Canvas API, browser-image-compression library
  • Animations: Lottie for loading animations
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymised)
  • Advertising: Google AdSense

Open Source & Licenses

AI Mosaic Studio makes use of several open-source libraries and frameworks. We are grateful to the developers and communities behind these projects.
